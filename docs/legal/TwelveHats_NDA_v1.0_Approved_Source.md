### 1. PURPOSE

The Company is developing and commercializing TwelveHats, a proprietary software platform incorporating business management, customer relationship management, dispatch, accounting, estimating, artificial intelligence, automation, and related technologies.

Recipient may be granted limited access to TwelveHats solely for evaluating, learning, demonstrating, marketing, and selling the platform on behalf of the Company, as expressly authorized in writing (“Permitted Purpose”).

Recipient acknowledges that access may expose valuable confidential information, trade secrets, proprietary technology, commercial strategies, and intellectual property.

Access is conditional upon compliance with this Agreement.

### 2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

“Confidential Information” includes all nonpublic information disclosed or made accessible to Recipient, directly or indirectly, in any form, including:

**A. Software and Technology**

- TwelveHats source code, object code, software architecture, databases, algorithms, APIs, integrations, and technical documentation.
- Artificial intelligence systems, including Luminary, Beacon, LIA, and related AI agents, models, orchestration methods, prompts, workflows, configurations, and proprietary logic.
- Software functionality, unreleased features, development roadmaps, testing environments, prototypes, and demonstrations.
- System designs, user interfaces, business rules, automation methods, security architecture, and implementation techniques.

**B. Commercial Information**

- Pricing models, subscription plans, licensing structures, sales commissions, and proposed business arrangements.
- Business plans, marketing strategies, customer acquisition methods, revenue forecasts, and financial projections.
- Prospect lists, customer lists, vendor relationships, business contacts, and sales opportunities.
- Product positioning, competitive strategies, and expansion plans.

**C. Operational and Customer Information**

- Customer records, job histories, accounting information, estimates, invoices, employee information, and operational analytics.
- Confidential information belonging to customers, vendors, contractors, and business partners.
- Demonstration datasets, simulated scenarios, internal testing results, and proprietary training materials.

**D. Access and Security Information**

- Login credentials, authentication methods, access tokens, system configurations, internal URLs, security controls, and administrative procedures.

Confidential Information includes information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential based on its nature or the circumstances of disclosure, whether or not marked confidential.

### 3. CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-USE OBLIGATIONS

Recipient shall:

1. Maintain Confidential Information in strict confidence.
2. Use Confidential Information exclusively for the Permitted Purpose.
3. Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without the Company's prior written authorization.
4. Not use Confidential Information for personal commercial advantage or the benefit of any competitor or third party.
5. Protect Confidential Information using at least reasonable care and no less than the care Recipient uses to protect similarly sensitive information.
6. Promptly notify the Company of any actual or suspected unauthorized disclosure, access, loss, or misuse.
7. Cooperate reasonably in investigating and containing any security incident.
8. Not retain Confidential Information after access or authorization ends, except as required by law.

Recipient is responsible for compliance by any person to whom Recipient improperly discloses Confidential Information.

### 4. RESTRICTED SOFTWARE ACCESS

Any access to TwelveHats is personal, limited, revocable, nonexclusive, nontransferable, and restricted to the Permitted Purpose.

Without prior written authorization, Recipient shall not:

- Share credentials or permit another person to access Recipient's account.
- Access customer environments, databases, or administrative functions outside assigned permissions.
- Copy, export, download, scrape, or systematically extract proprietary information.
- Reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or attempt to discover nonpublic source code or algorithms, except to the extent applicable law expressly prohibits such a restriction.
- Circumvent access controls, security restrictions, logging, or tenant isolation.
- Conduct penetration testing or security probing without written authorization.
- Create unauthorized integrations or connect unapproved external services.
- Upload Confidential Information to public or third-party AI systems without written authorization.
- Record demonstrations, capture screenshots, or distribute platform materials without written authorization.
- Use access to create, train, improve, or assist a competing software product using Confidential Information.

Recipient shall use only Company-approved demonstration accounts and environments.

The Company may suspend or revoke access at any time, subject to applicable law and any separate written agreement.

### 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY OWNERSHIP

All rights, title, and interest in TwelveHats and the Company's preexisting or independently developed intellectual property remain exclusively with the Company or its applicable licensors.

Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership or grants Recipient any license other than the limited access rights expressly provided.

Recipient shall not claim ownership of the Company's software, algorithms, product concepts, documentation, trademarks, trade secrets, or proprietary information.

Recipient shall not register or attempt to register any confusingly similar domain name, trademark, or other intellectual property right based on the Company's Confidential Information.

### 6. WORK PRODUCT AND IMPROVEMENTS

To the extent Recipient is separately authorized to create sales materials, product documentation, demonstrations, software modifications, designs, or other deliverables specifically for the Company, ownership of those deliverables shall be governed by the Parties' applicable written employment, contractor, or intellectual property assignment agreement.

Recipient agrees to disclose such Company-directed work product and execute reasonably necessary documents to confirm the Company's ownership rights where those rights have been validly assigned.

This Agreement does not automatically transfer Recipient's independently developed, preexisting intellectual property or inventions protected by applicable law.

### 7. PROHIBITION ON MISAPPROPRIATION AND COMPETITIVE MISUSE

Recipient shall not use or disclose the Company's Confidential Information or trade secrets to develop, market, operate, finance, advise, or assist any competing product or business.

Recipient shall not provide the Company's proprietary architecture, source code, confidential business strategies, customer information, or technical methods to competitors.

This provision prohibits misuse of protected information. It does not independently prohibit lawful employment, general competition, or the use of skills and knowledge that do not constitute protected Confidential Information.

Any separate noncompetition or nonsolicitation restriction must be established in a distinct, legally enforceable written agreement.

### 8. CUSTOMER AND PROSPECT INFORMATION

All nonpublic prospect information, customer records, sales opportunities, and business contact information obtained through the Company's systems shall be used only for authorized Company business.

Recipient shall not:

- Export customer or prospect databases for personal use.
- Transfer Company leads or opportunities to another business.
- Use confidential customer information to divert business opportunities.
- Contact customers or prospects on behalf of another business using Confidential Information acquired through TwelveHats.

Recipient shall comply with applicable privacy, consumer-protection, and data-security laws.

### 9. DEMONSTRATIONS AND REPRESENTATIONS

Recipient may demonstrate only features and functionality authorized by the Company.

Recipient shall not:

- Represent fictional demonstration data as actual customer records or verified business performance.
- Misrepresent scripted AI responses as live AI analysis.
- Promise unreleased capabilities, implementation dates, performance guarantees, or contractual commitments without authorization.
- Offer unauthorized pricing, discounts, warranties, or contractual terms.
- Grant third parties access to TwelveHats without Company approval.

All demonstrations using synthetic information must be identified appropriately as demonstrations.

### 10. SECURITY AND INCIDENT REPORTING

Recipient shall:

- Use strong passwords and multifactor authentication where required.
- Maintain reasonable device and account security.
- Avoid storing credentials or Confidential Information on unauthorized devices or services.
- Immediately report suspected credential compromise or unauthorized system access.
- Follow reasonable written Company security policies supplied to Recipient.

Recipient shall notify the Company without undue delay, and in any event within 24 hours after discovering a suspected security incident involving Company information or systems, unless prohibited by law.

### 11. EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

Confidential Information does not include information Recipient can demonstrate through reliable evidence:

1. Became publicly available without breach of this Agreement.
2. Was lawfully known to Recipient before disclosure and without confidentiality restrictions.
3. Was lawfully obtained from a third party without a duty of confidentiality.
4. Was independently developed without use of or reference to the Company's Confidential Information.
5. Was expressly released for unrestricted disclosure by the Company in writing.

### 12. LEGALLY PROTECTED DISCLOSURES

Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Recipient from:

- Reporting suspected violations of law to government agencies or regulators.
- Cooperating with lawful government investigations.
- Making disclosures protected by whistleblower laws.
- Discussing legally protected workplace matters.
- Exercising rights under applicable labor or employment laws.
- Making disclosures otherwise required or protected by law.

Where legally permitted, Recipient shall provide reasonable notice of compelled disclosure and disclose only the information legally required.

No prior Company approval is required for protected reports to government authorities.

**Federal Trade Secret Immunity Notice — 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b)**

An individual shall not be held criminally or civilly liable under federal or state trade secret law for disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official, directly or indirectly, or to an attorney, solely for reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law; or made in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding.

An individual bringing a retaliation lawsuit for reporting a suspected violation of law may disclose the trade secret to the individual's attorney and use it in the court proceeding if documents containing the trade secret are filed under seal and the trade secret is not otherwise disclosed except pursuant to court order.

### 13. TERM AND SURVIVAL

This Agreement becomes effective upon signature and applies to Confidential Information disclosed before or after the Effective Date in connection with the Permitted Purpose.

Confidentiality and non-use obligations concerning ordinary Confidential Information shall continue for five years after the later of termination of access or the last authorized disclosure.

Obligations concerning legally protected trade secrets shall continue for as long as the information qualifies for trade-secret protection under applicable law.

Obligations concerning personal information, credentials, and information subject to independent legal confidentiality requirements shall continue for the period required by applicable law.

Intellectual property ownership provisions survive termination.

### 14. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

Upon Company request or termination of access, Recipient shall promptly:

1. Stop using Company systems.
2. Return Company-owned equipment and materials.
3. Return or securely delete Confidential Information from personal devices, accounts, and storage.
4. Delete unauthorized copies, exports, recordings, and screenshots.
5. Provide written confirmation of compliance upon reasonable request.

Legally required retained copies may be preserved only to the extent required by law and shall remain protected.

### 15. EQUITABLE RELIEF AND REMEDIES

Recipient acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure or misuse of Confidential Information may cause substantial harm that monetary damages alone may not adequately remedy.

Subject to applicable law, the Company may seek temporary, preliminary, or permanent injunctive relief, specific performance, damages, and other available remedies.

The Company need not waive any available remedy by pursuing another.

Any bond requirement, evidentiary standard, or other condition for equitable relief shall be determined under applicable law.

### 16. ATTORNEYS' FEES AND COSTS

In an action to enforce this Agreement, the prevailing Party may recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs to the extent permitted by applicable law and ordered by the court.

### 17. GOVERNING LAW AND VENUE

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

Subject to applicable jurisdictional and venue requirements, disputes arising from this Agreement shall be brought in the appropriate state courts located in Pinellas County, Florida, or the applicable federal court serving that jurisdiction.

### 18. NO EMPLOYMENT OR COMPENSATION GUARANTEE

This Agreement does not create an employment relationship, independent contractor relationship, commission entitlement, partnership, franchise, or guarantee of continued system access.

Any compensation, commissions, sales territory, employment status, or independent contractor terms shall be governed by a separate written agreement.

### 19. ENTIRE AGREEMENT AND SEVERABILITY

This Agreement constitutes the Parties' agreement concerning confidentiality and restricted system access, except to the extent supplemented by another executed written agreement.

If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain effective to the extent permitted by law.

Electronic signatures and counterparts shall be permitted to the extent authorized by applicable law.
