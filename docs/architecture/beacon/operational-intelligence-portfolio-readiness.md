# Beacon operational intelligence portfolio readiness

This checkpoint qualifies Beacon as a deterministic, advisory operator-intelligence
product. Source domains retain all business mutation authority. Beacon never treats
acknowledgement, ownership, snooze, priority, or escalation presentation as proof that
the source condition was corrected.

## Security and authority correction

Operational fact loading is now constrained to the principal's authorized Branch set,
or the explicitly active Branch. The authorization-scope digest participates in signal
and condition identity, preventing one Branch-scoped workflow history from colliding
with another scope that happens to contain identical facts. Company isolation remains
mandatory at every query and workflow boundary.

## LIA-ready evidence contract

`BEACON.INTELLIGENCE.v1` returns only an already-admitted signal visible to the
requesting ACP principal. It binds Company/Branch, definition and evidence identity,
quality, priority, lifecycle, owner, escalation readiness, safe source references, and
the exact actions available under the principal's Beacon permissions. Identical inputs
produce the same packet digest. It grants no database access, reasoning authority,
source-domain permission, or autonomous execution.

## Truthful unresolved gates

Unknown Company priority/escalation values remain configurable and visibly
`UNCONFIGURED`; they do not prevent architecture readiness. Product completion cannot
truthfully claim source-fact resolution, historical signal-volume reporting, deferred
financial/economic signals, or notification delivery until their respective authority
contracts are accepted. These omissions are fail-closed and must not be replaced with
hidden defaults.
