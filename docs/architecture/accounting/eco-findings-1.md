# ECO.FINDINGS.1 — Evidence-bound economic inconsistency findings

Business Economics now projects provider-neutral source assertions into immutable,
deterministic readiness and inconsistency findings. An owning domain must supply the
subject identity, reconciliation key, and required components explicitly. Description
text, vendor names, and temporal proximity never create Job or service-line linkage.

The finding families cover Job and service-line readiness; revenue, settlement, and
material inconsistencies; labor and overhead readiness; and potential margin-leakage
conditions. States are `READY`, `PARTIAL`, `ABSENT`, `CONFLICTING`, and `UNKNOWN`.
Missing evidence remains absent rather than zero. Conflicting assertions retain every
authority and digest without applying source precedence.

Each finding includes its definition version, deterministic identity, subject and
reconciliation identities, component, state, confidence, measured condition, source
authorities, immutable evidence/value/package digests, limitations, and safe factual
explanations. Identical inputs produce identical findings regardless of evaluation
order.

Potential leakage means that evidence establishes a gap or disagreement capable of
blocking reliable margin analysis. It does not assert a dollar loss, compute profit,
select accounting policy, allocate overhead, burden labor, cost materials, recognize
revenue, or authorize remediation. QBO remains source-reported and unaccepted.

Beacon may later signal a validated finding. Luminary may explain it and recommend
options, and LIA may guide an authorized resolution. Those behaviors and Accounting
corrections remain outside this milestone.
