# QBO → OM2-B protected source evidence v1

OM1 owns sanctioned QuickBooks Online connection, GET-only acquisition, immutable
source envelopes, pagination evidence, control registrations, and this handoff.
OM2-B may build financial read models from the packet; it does not receive OAuth
secrets, raw Customer payloads, a new Customer master, or ledger-posting authority.

`qbo-om2b-source-evidence/v1` distinguishes `live`, `historical`, and `blocked`.
Only a production CompanyInfo identity match plus a sealed terminal production
manifest may be labeled `live`. A prior report/control registration remains
`historical` even when its contents are authoritative for its stated period.

The packet preserves a hashed provider/company identity, CompanyInfo verification
time, API minor version, acquisition start/end, accounting cutoff and timezone,
manifest digest, per-entity counts, per-entity page counts, provider catalog
dispositions, report basis/end/generated timestamps, and explicit limitations.
Raw provider data remains in the protected content-addressed evidence store.

Financial records are QBO source-reported assertions pending Enterprise acceptance.
Payments do not become revenue; purchases do not become Job material consumption;
QBO Employee/TimeActivity does not establish Payroll or productive labor; report
basis is never inferred; and no packet authorizes policy, posting, repricing,
money movement, Luminary action, or employment action.
