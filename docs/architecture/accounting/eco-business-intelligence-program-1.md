# Business Economics → Luminary owner-intelligence program

This program composes authoritative Economics, Luminary, Beacon, and LIA
contracts; it does not create another analytics ledger. The runtime
`economics.capability-readiness.v1` endpoint is the machine-readable capability
audit and deliberately identifies policy, source, external, dependency, and
active-owner gates rather than flattening them into completion.

## Authoritative product boundary

Current authority supplies immutable admitted profitability results, Job,
service/category, Customer and Branch rollups, equal-duration period comparison,
policy administration, overhead allocation evidence, immutable correction
lineage, deterministic owner questions, Luminary findings and briefings, Beacon
references, and permission-aware LIA retrieval. Contribution is not Accounting
net income. Payment is not revenue. An Estimate is not realized revenue.

The source-completeness matrix now covers revenue, settlement, labor, employer
burden, materials, other direct costs, allocation, Job/Customer/category/Branch
attribution, workforce attribution, procurement provenance, callback/warranty,
Service Agreements, capacity, cash/working capital, and Accounting. Its bounded
exception center orders conflicting and stale evidence ahead of policy and
source gaps. It has no mutation authority.

## Comparison and interpretation

Canonical day, week, calendar month, calendar quarter, calendar year, and custom
period contracts reject invalid boundaries. Comparisons report incompatible
period, currency, or policy versions instead of producing a misleading change.
Owner questions cover Job/service/Branch contribution, labor and material
movement, incomplete measurements, full-profitability blockers, owner decisions,
and inspection priority. Component movement is an observation, never a causal
claim.

Luminary adds a single deterministic source-readiness finding to its briefing,
retains Economics evidence digests, and points to the first owning-domain
inspections. Beacon keeps signal lifecycle. LIA keeps read-only explanation and
navigation authority and cannot select policy or mutate a domain.

## Remaining gates

- Real cost pools, allocation drivers, rates, scope, and effective dates remain
  owner finance-policy decisions.
- Callback/warranty requires an authoritative corrective-work relationship from
  Jobs/Assets. Labels and free text are insufficient.
- Workforce economics requires explicit protected Employee-to-Job attribution.
- Service Agreement economics requires admitted recognition and entitlement-
  consumption evidence; enrollment or billing readiness is not revenue.
- Capacity economics requires accepted capacity, schedule, completion, and labor
  measurement contracts, with no invented target.
- Cash and working-capital intelligence requires admitted native Accounting
  evidence. Real-QBO acquisition remains exclusively Migration-owned.

No real Company policy, QBO evidence, Accounting mutation, operational mutation,
Preview deployment, or Production operation is authorized by this program.
