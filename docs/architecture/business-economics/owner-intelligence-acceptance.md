# Owner intelligence acceptance

Status: non-production acceptance contract. This document does not admit new economic evidence or configure Company policy.

## The three truths

ACP presents three related but independent views:

1. **Work performed** is admitted Business Economics evidence for the work period. It remains visible when collection happens later.
2. **Still owed** is operational customer or Vendor obligation evidence. It is neither cash nor Accounting recognition.
3. **Cash-basis Accounting** is admitted Accounting evidence for the reporting period. An Invoice, Payment assertion, settlement, or deposit never substitutes for it.

Same-day payment, Net-30, partial and late payment, Vendor terms, card purchase, and later card settlement must preserve these distinctions. A later collection or card payment changes settlement/cash evidence, not historical Job contribution or material cost. A true zero, no records, missing evidence, missing policy, stale evidence, conflicting evidence, and unauthorized evidence are different states.

## Owner-question acceptance

The workspace exposes `economics.owner-question-acceptance.v1`. Every supported question is classified as `ANSWERABLE`, `PARTIALLY_ANSWERABLE`, `POLICY_REQUIRED`, `SOURCE_REQUIRED`, `EXTERNAL_GATE`, `NOT_AUTHORIZED`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`. Each row names its owning domains, safe inspection path, limitation, and resolution. Its digest is deterministic and its mutation authority is `none`.

The inventory covers today/week/month, period change, performed work, collection, completed work still unpaid, Job/service/Branch contribution, labor and material movement, Vendor obligations, profit/cash divergence, missing/stale/conflicting evidence, owner decisions, inspection priority, and current unknowns. Cash totals remain externally gated until admitted Accounting reporting evidence exists. Permission failure is classified before protected retrieval.

## Explanation and causality

An accepted explanation states what changed, measured direction/amount when admitted, cited evidence, limitations, and the next inspection. It may say “associated with,” “coincided with,” or “contributed through this measured component.” It must not claim that an Employee, Vendor, marketing activity, callback, or other correlated condition caused an outcome without causal authority.

The progressive-disclosure path is summary → readiness/limitation → accepted result → source/admission/package/computation digests → policy/allocation version → immutable predecessor/successor history. Job results may identify their authoritative Job; Customer, category, and Branch aggregates must not synthesize foreign identities.

The reusable acceptance scenario contains only synthetic evidence:

- an open Vendor obligation;
- a card material purchase and later liability settlement;

Acceptance proves that work remains visible before collection, obligations do not become cash, settlement is not a second cost, incomplete evidence is not zero, unsupported causes are refused, old results remain inspectable, and phone-width content retains status and limitation text.

## Future real-company acceptance script

Using an authorized owner account and a bounded reporting period:

1. Open Business Economics and identify work performed, still owed, and cash-basis Accounting. Explain why they differ.
2. Review evidence coverage and name every partial, stale, conflicting, policy-required, and external-gate state.
3. Ask what changed, which Jobs/services contributed most and least, and where labor/material costs moved. Open the cited result and verify lineage.
4. Review the Luminary briefing. For each priority finding, identify observation, evidence, limitation, and next inspection. Reject any unsupported causal statement.
5. Inspect correction history and confirm both predecessor and current result remain available.
6. Follow Ask LIA and Beacon links; verify neither handoff recalculates truth, creates a signal, or grants mutation authority.
7. Repeat at desktop, tablet, and phone width, then revoke a permission and verify protected evidence disappears without revealing existence.
