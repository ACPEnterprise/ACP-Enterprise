# Accountant cutover packet: historical Cash basis

## What is being preserved

All County's historical financial-reporting basis is **Cash**. ACP will preserve
that policy without discarding unpaid customer or vendor obligations.

- An issued Invoice, its terms, due date, applications, and open balance are
  operational AR even before cash-basis income recognition.
- A Vendor Bill, terms, credits, payments, and open balance are operational AP
  even before cash-basis expense recognition.
- A credit-card Purchase and the later card payment are different events: the
  first establishes the purchase/card obligation; the second establishes bank
  settlement.
- Bank balances come from bank/account controls, not totals of Payments or
  Deposits. Undeposited funds remains a separate clearing control.
- Business Economics consumes accepted earned-work and attributable-cost
  evidence. It does not treat payment timing as whether a Job economically
  existed or was profitable.

## No double counting

Invoices establish customer obligations, not cash. Payments establish receipt
and application evidence, not a second revenue event. Deposits move or settle
cash/clearing and are not automatically revenue. Purchases establish expense or
asset/card/payable evidence; later bank or card settlement is not a second
expense. Journals are admitted only with source lineage preventing duplicate
recognition of their originating transaction.
