# Beacon Real Owner Operability 1

## Owner workflow

The authenticated Command Center is the owner entry point. Beacon appears
immediately after the operating-scope selector and before the financial and
operational scorecards. It preserves the active Company/Branch authorization
context and refreshes its read-only evaluation every minute.

Each card explicitly separates:

- **Measured facts** from authoritative source records;
- **Interpretation** from deterministic priority rules; and
- **Recommended human action**, which Beacon does not execute.

Evidence links open the exact Job, Appointment, or Invoice source workflow.
Evidence time, relevance expiration, completeness, freshness, reconciliation,
confidence, limitations, ownership, snooze, and review history remain visible.

## Recommendation coverage

The owner surface consumes Beacon's existing cross-domain adapter registry.
It distinguishes active families from adapter-, source-, or policy-gated
families. An empty active queue therefore means no configured active condition
was measured; it does not imply that unevaluated labor, Economics, Payroll,
Accounting, Inventory, or capacity conditions are healthy.

Current active real-record evaluation covers:

- overdue committed Appointments;
- paused Jobs; and
- past-due open Invoices.

Current protected contracts still gate broader conclusions, including Job
labor consumption, Parts Run loss, expected-duration variance, immutable
Economics/Luminary findings, source completeness, Workforce/timekeeping,
Payroll readiness, Accounting controls, Price Book, Estimates, Inventory, and
capacity. Their exact source authority, responsibility, clearing condition,
and limitation are presented without fabricating a recommendation.

## Real-data acceptance boundary

The Beta endpoint is reachable and correctly rejects unauthenticated Beacon
reads. This worker had no sanctioned owner session, so no All County record was
read and no claim is made about the current real signal population. After
protected integration and Beta deployment, Enterprise should authenticate as
an authorized owner and verify:

1. Beacon is visible at the top of Command Center.
2. Company-wide and selected-Branch queues remain correctly scoped.
3. Each active card links to its exact source record.
4. Measured fact, interpretation, and recommended action remain distinct.
5. Snooze/review/ownership actions refresh without resolving source truth.
6. An empty queue still displays recommendation coverage and gated families.

No Production deployment, source-domain mutation, autonomous action, pricing
change, staffing change, Dispatch change, or financial-policy change is part of
this candidate.
