# Luminary real owner operability

## Owner acceptance path

1. Sign in to Twelve Hats Beta with `COMPANY_LUMINARY_READ`.
2. Open **Luminary** from the authorized navigation.
3. Select **Today**, **This week**, **This month**, or **This year**.
4. Read **Where the business stands** before opening technical evidence.
5. Confirm Revenue Production states its basis, Economic Contribution does not
   include burden, Required Economic Burden is unavailable unless an approved
   same-period allocation exists, and Economic Health is unavailable unless
   both numerator and denominator are authoritative.
6. Open **Evidence and missing burden inputs** and confirm every unavailable
   burden family is named. Cash Health must remain separate.
7. Review the equal-period comparison and measured driver explanation. A driver
   is arithmetic evidence, not a causal claim.
8. Drill into a supporting Job and use **Ask LIA about this evidence** for the
   read-only conversational path.

## Real-data rerun

For each period, record the response packet digest, generated-at timestamp,
currency, evidence classification, admitted reference count, missing evidence,
and comparison state. Compare visible values to the owning Business Economics
workspace for the same Company, Branch, and exact period. Do not accept a
burden or health percentage when `fully_allocated_available` is false.

## Current evidence boundary

ACP can project accepted native Invoice, Job, worked-interval, material, and
settlement evidence without converting absence to zero. Direct contribution
requires admitted immutable Economics results. Required burden additionally
requires complete same-period overhead results produced under approved pool and
allocation policy. Until those records exist for real All County periods,
Economic Health remains truthfully unavailable; no industry or synthetic
assumption is substituted.

This increment is read-only, adds no persistence or schema, and has no Cash,
Accounting, Payroll, pricing, or operational mutation authority.
