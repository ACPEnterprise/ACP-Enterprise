# Luminary active economic reasoning 3

## Owner interpretation

For a selected period, the owner projection now separates:

- **Observed change:** exact equal-period movement.
- **Measured driver:** the arithmetic contribution effect of an admitted
  component.
- **Possible driver:** a bounded investigation lead, such as changed Job mix.
- **Unproven cause:** any explanation not established by owning-domain evidence.

Materiality is shown as exact currency/count movement, percentage of the prior
period, affected Job population, affected authoritative invoiced population,
and contribution effect. Luminary does not assign subjective severity or a
dollar value to missing evidence.

## Supported measured movement

When both equal periods are complete and comparable, the response covers:

- Revenue Production;
- Job count;
- average produced revenue per Job;
- Economic Contribution;
- total admitted Job-variable cost;
- direct labor cost;
- direct material cost;
- material cost per Job.

Payroll burden, fixed/semi-fixed burden, and marketing spend remain unknown
unless their canonical same-period values are admitted. AR and collections are
explicitly excluded from Economic Health and retained under Cash Health.

## Break-even guidance

When Economic Health is unavailable, the response retains the known numerator,
known denominator, numerator gaps, denominator gaps, highest-value dependency,
responsible party, expected source, and safe UI route. When it is available,
the response adds the exact percentage and distance in percentage points from
100% break-even.

## Beta revision

The read-only evaluation is recomputed from current admitted evidence on every
request. Its deterministic packet digest changes with evidence. Existing
Luminary finding and briefing lineage remains historical evidence; stale inputs
stay labeled and are never silently promoted to current truth.

No schema, persistence, source-domain calculation, autonomous recommendation,
pricing action, Payroll action, or Production deployment is included.
