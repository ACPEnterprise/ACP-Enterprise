# Luminary active economic reasoning 2

## Contract boundary

Luminary interprets the canonical Business Economics workspace. It does not
calculate source-domain costs, select allocation policy, estimate missing
values, or persist recommendations.

Evidence gaps are ordered by:

1. the management conclusion they block;
2. the number of authoritative Jobs affected when a Job population exists;
3. a stable gap key solely as a deterministic tie-breaker.

This is dependency priority, not an estimated dollar impact. Categories without
authoritative values are never assigned relative monetary weight.

## Fixture-only owner example

Given a fixture with $3,000.00 measured Revenue Production, $1,150.00 measured
Economic Contribution, no admitted Required Economic Burden, and two of two
Jobs complete for direct contribution, Luminary reports:

> Revenue Production and Economic Contribution are available for this period.
> Required Economic Burden is unavailable, so I cannot state whether the
> business is above or below break-even. The first unresolved dependency is
> field capacity burden. The expected path is Payroll -> First real Payroll
> readiness. Completing it makes field capacity burden available to the
> Required Economic Burden model.

If only one of two Jobs has complete admitted variable costs, Luminary labels
Economic Contribution `PARTIAL`, reports 50% **Job-population coverage**, and
does not show the partial contribution as a Company total.

## Period interpretation

Today, week-to-date, month-to-date, and year-to-date use the existing exact
period contract. Prior-period explanations remain limited to equal-length,
single-authority comparisons. Component changes are classified as observed
changes and arithmetic contribution effects; they are never promoted to proven
operational causes.
